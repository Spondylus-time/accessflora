The Oyster

Certainty, on the half shell.

Wild and farmed oysters vary by tide, season, and source. SureSweet oysters are conditioned after harvest so you get the same profile in every order, whenever you order it.

Salinity

Dialed in, not left to chance

Each batch is conditioned to a target salinity, from a clean 10 ppt to a bold 35 ppt, instead of whatever the tide delivered that week.

Sweetness

Full flavor, every batch

A curated microalgae diet fattens the oyster for a rounder, sweeter finish, tuned from standard to sweetest.

Clarity

Sediment-free

Purified water means no grit and no mud, so oysters shuck perfectly clean and eat smooth, with none of the grittiness that comes from sediment in the shell.

Safety

Every batch tested

Every batch is lab-tested for safety before it ships. Certainty isn't a promise on a menu, it's a process behind it.

How It Works

From the farm to a known flavor.

We don't grow oysters from seed. We take good oysters and make them predictable.

01 · Source

Start with quality

We source oysters from trusted external farms as our raw material.

02 · Condition

Purified water, curated diet

Oysters spend time in our recirculating aquaculture system on a controlled microalgae diet.

03 · Dial In

Tune the profile

Salinity and sweetness are set to a target, fattening the oyster for consistent, full flavor.

04 · Hold Fresh

Wait on us, not the calendar

Finished oysters stay held in water until the day of your delivery, so every order arrives just-harvested fresh, not days off the boat.

05 · Test & Ship

Verify, then send

Every batch is tested for safety before it reaches your kitchen.
