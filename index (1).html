Meet The Founder

Farming, nutrition, and oyster sales.

Hi, I'm Keegan. During my graduate studies, I learned aquaculture firsthand — from Australia and Ecuador to right here in Connecticut — which led me to launch a mobile raw bar selling oysters.

Along the way, I saw a growing vulnerability: ocean unpredictability was making our oyster supply inconsistent. So I created AccessFlora. Using a proprietary natural aquafeed, we raise oysters that are safe, rich in Omega-3s, and fully controlled in flavor — meaning year-round consistency.
